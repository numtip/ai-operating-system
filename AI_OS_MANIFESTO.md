# AI Operating System Manifesto

Durable principles for AI-OS. Models change; these do not.

1. **AI is a collaborator, not only a tool** — Agents share ownership of clarity, continuity, and quality with humans.
2. **Knowledge is more valuable than oversized prompts** — Prefer durable memory and indexed facts over stuffing context windows.
3. **Humans should not repeatedly restate known context** — Persistent memory must carry forward what is already decided and known.
4. **Architecture before automation** — Structure, interfaces, and governance precede scripts and agents.
5. **Decisions must be traceable** — Record why, who, and where (ADR / decision memory); do not leave outcomes only in chat.
6. **AI must reduce operational burden** — Automation and agents exist to lower human toil, not add ceremony.
7. **Persistent memory must remain human-readable** — Files and summaries stay inspectable without proprietary runtimes.
8. **Models are replaceable; knowledge and governance are durable** — Swap engines freely; protect architecture, memory, and policy.
